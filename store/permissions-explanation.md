# Permissions explanation
- CAMERA: capture photos/videos (core feature).
- RECORD_AUDIO: video sound only; requested when entering video recording.
- ACCESS_FINE/COARSE_LOCATION: optional GPS tagging for map/journey; media saves without it.
- READ_MEDIA_IMAGES/VIDEO + ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: show/save captures.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: journey export progress only.
- USB host (implied): external SSD recording where a volume is mounted.
